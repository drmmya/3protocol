Before you connect a domain

  1. Point your domain’s A record at this VPS. Remove incorrect AAAA records.
  2. Allow inbound TCP 80 and 9443 in your provider firewall.
  3. Enter your domain here and install the certificate.
  4. Export updated VPN profiles after switching to domain/TLS.
IP-only installation uses a self-signed certificate. Compare its fingerprint with the installer output before trusting it.
